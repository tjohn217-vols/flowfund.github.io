About FlowFund

FlowFund is an AI-powered financial platform that automatically adjusts your spending, saving, and investing based on life changes like new jobs, rent increases, or financial goals.

How It Works

  1. Connect your financial accounts
  2. Set your goals
  3. FlowFund dynamically adjusts your finances in real time

Key Features

Dynamic Budgeting

Automatically updates your budget as your income and expenses change.

Life Event AI

Adapts your financial plan instantly when major life events occur.

Goal-Based Investing

Invests your money based on goals like buying a house or retirement.

Financial Simulation

Test scenarios like moving cities or changing jobs.
