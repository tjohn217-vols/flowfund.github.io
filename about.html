About FlowFund

FlowFund was created by a graduate student at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, with a passion for budgeting, financial optimization, and maximizing economic potential.

The goal of FlowFund is simple: to make financial decision-making dynamic, intelligent, and accessible to everyone.

Traditional budgeting tools are static. FlowFund introduces a new paradigm— real-time financial optimization powered by AI.
